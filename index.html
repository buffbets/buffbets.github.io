How it works

Meet the line reader.

The bull in the suit is the bot. He reads ten sportsbooks and exchanges all day, works out what each bet is really worth, and speaks up when one of your books pays more than that. He doesn't pick winners. He reads prices.

How he knows a price is too good.

Every book takes a cut. The bot starts with Pinnacle, the sharp book other bettors measure against, takes its cut out, and on pre-game moneylines blends in Kalshi's exchange price. That's the fair price. When one of your books pays more, that's a +EV bet. Here's the Rams example, worked out.

  1. Step 1 · Read the sharp book102.8%

    Pinnacle has the Rams at +131 and the Seahawks at −147. Turn those into win chances and they add up to 102.8%. The extra 2.8% is Pinnacle's cut.

    +131 → 43.3% · −147 → 59.5% · total 102.8%
  2. Step 2 · Take the cut out41.8%

    The bot removes the cut with the power method, which trims the underdog a little more than the favorite.

    Rams 43.3% → 41.8%
  3. Step 3 · Check the exchange42.0%

    Before the game, it blends in Kalshi, an exchange where bettors trade with each other. Kalshi has the Rams at 42.5%. Blended fair price: 42.0%, or +138 in odds.

    Pinnacle 41.8% + Kalshi 42.5% → 42.0% = +138
  4. Step 4 · Compare your books+4.0%

    FanDuel pays +148. A 42% shot paid at +148 gives back about $104 for every $100 bet, on average. That extra 4% is the edge. It clears the 4% bar, so the alert goes out.

    +148 vs fair +138 → edge 4.0% → alert posted

Before you join

Do I need to be good at math?

No. Each alert says what to bet, where, the odds and how much. The math is done before it reaches your phone.

Will I win every bet?

No. A +EV bet is a good price, not a sure thing. A 42% shot still loses 58% of the time. The edge shows up over hundreds of bets, so keep stakes small and be patient.

Which books do I need?

Alerts point to FanDuel, DraftKings, BetMGM, Caesars and Kalshi. The more of them you have accounts at, the more alerts you can act on. Pinnacle, BetOnline, LowVig, Novig and ProphetX are only used as reference prices.

How fast are the alerts?

Upcoming games are checked all day, and player props every 3 minutes in the 8 hours before the game. Alerts are for pre-game bets. Good prices can move fast, so turn on notifications.

What does GONE mean?

The price disappeared. The bot edits the card to GONE and says how long it lasted, so nobody chases it.

What's a unit?

1% of your bankroll. If you set aside $1,000 for betting, one unit is $10, so a 1.5u stake is $15.

Is Buff Bets a sportsbook?

No. We don't take bets or hold money. We send information; you place every bet yourself at your own accounts.
